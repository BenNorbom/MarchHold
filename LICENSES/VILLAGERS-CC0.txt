MarchHold villagers use optimized versions of Grab3D's Medieval Villager, Farmer Woman
and Child, including the accompanying textures, rig and walk/run animations.
Grab3D publishes these assets under CC0 1.0 Universal.

https://grab3d.com/models/characters/medieval-villager/
https://grab3d.com/models/characters/farmer-woman/
https://grab3d.com/models/characters/child/
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

MarchHold adaptations include mesh simplification, clothing masks, body variations,
work poses, resource-carry poses and equipment attachments.
